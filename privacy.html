Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This website is the personal CV of Grigoris Kazakou. This page explains, in plain language, what data is and is not collected when you visit it.

In short: no cookies, no advertising, no personal profiling. This site only counts anonymous visits so I can see how many people view my CV — never who they are.

What is collected

This site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly, open-source analytics tool, to count page views. It records only aggregated, anonymous information such as:

  • The page viewed and the time of the visit
  • Approximate country (derived momentarily, not stored with your identity)
  • Browser type and whether the visit came from a link (referrer)

What is NOT collected

  • No cookies are placed on your device
  • No IP addresses are stored
  • No names, emails, or personal identifiers
  • No cross-site tracking or advertising profiles

Legal basis (GDPR)

Because the analytics used here do not collect personal data as defined by the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), no personal data about visitors is processed, stored, or shared. No consent is strictly required; a notice is provided here for transparency.

Your rights

As no personal data is stored, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. If you have any questions about this site, you can contact me at the email address shown on the CV.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect limited technical logs as part of serving the page; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.
